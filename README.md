## Features

-  **Up to 1,000 CPS** with live speed adjustment *(unlike most autoclickers the CPS selected are 99% accurate)*.
-  **Two click modes:** click at your cursor or cycle through up to 64 screen positions.
-  **Custom hotkeys** for starting, stopping, hiding, and more *(hiding will hide it from your taskbar as well)*.
-  **Cat Mode:** shrink the window into a tiny kitty.
-  **Performance Mode:** a minimal interface with fewer animations.
-  **Customizable appearance:** transparency, opening animation, and window position.
-  **Saved settings:** your preferences are remembered between sessions.
